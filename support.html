Common questions

How do I log in? Tap Instagram on Lowtide's home screen and log in on Instagram's own page with your username, email or phone number and password. Log in with Facebook and passkeys aren't available inside Lowtide.

How do I get back to Lowtide's home screen? Swipe up from the bottom of the screen once. Swipe up again to leave Lowtide.

Can I turn a filter off? Yes: tap the gear on Lowtide's home screen and switch any of them off.

Do I still get notifications? Lowtide doesn't send notifications. Keep the Instagram app installed with notifications on if you want them.

What does Lowtide know about me? Nothing. See the privacy policy.
