In short

Lowtide never knows your name, email or phone number. If you use a school or company login, we keep only a random code per phone, to count places.

What we collect

Without a school or company login: nothing. Lowtide has no personal account and receives nothing about you.

School and company login

A school or company can buy places for Lowtide. You then log in in the app with the school or company name and a shared password. Each phone that logs in takes one place.

  • What we keep: a code that the app makes at random on your phone, stored only as a one-way hash (no name, email, phone number or hardware id), the date the phone logged in, and a hash of the login session. After a wrong password we count failed tries per phone for up to one day.
  • Why: to give access until the end date and to make sure no more phones log in than the places bought. We use it for nothing else.
  • Who sees what: the school or company only sees how many places are in use and the end date. Not who, and not what you do in Lowtide or on Instagram.
  • How long: until 30 days after the school's or company's end date, then it is deleted automatically. A new password from the school or company removes all codes at once.
  • Roles: for a school, the school board decides on this and we process the code on its behalf under a data processing agreement. For a company, we do the same for the company.
  • Deleting: log out in Lowtide and delete the app, and ask your school or company or us to remove the place. Because we don't know which code is yours, we can't look you up by name.

Ordering for a school or company

When a school or company orders places, we receive the organisation's name, Chamber of Commerce number and the contact and invoice email addresses it gives us. We use them to set up the login, send the login and invoices, and keep our accounts. Invoices and order details are kept for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.

Services we use

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) stores the login data above in a database in the EU [region: to fill in when the project is created]. Supabase is a US company; its data processing agreement with the EU standard contractual clauses applies.
  • Resend (Resend, Inc.) sends emails about orders, logins and invoices to the organisation's contact address. It does not receive any code from phones. Resend is a US company; its data processing agreement with the EU standard contractual clauses applies.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website (see Updates below).

Instagram

You log in on Instagram's own page, with your own Instagram account. Your login and everything you do on Instagram go between your phone and Instagram. Lowtide receives none of it. Instagram (Meta) does handle your account and what you do there, under its own terms and privacy policy: privacycenter.instagram.com/policy. The same goes for YouTube (Google) and LinkedIn.

On your phone

Your Instagram login and your Lowtide settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting Lowtide removes them.

Updates

Lowtide checks this website for update notices. The request contains no personal data. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no cookies.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold. Email himbelton@gmail.com. You can also contact the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Lowtide is rated 16+ in the App Store and Google Play. Younger users should only use it with their parent's or guardian's permission, and only if they are old enough for Instagram (13).
