Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

Lowtide is an iPhone app made by Max Zijlstra, a sole trader in the Netherlands trading as Lowtide ("Lowtide", "we", "us"). Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the controller of the little personal data described below.

  • Address: TRADER_ADDRESS
  • Chamber of Commerce (KvK): KVK_NUMBER
  • Email: himbelton@gmail.com

This policy covers the Lowtide app and the Lowtide website (himbeltonmax.github.io/lowtide-site). It explains what happens to your data, and what doesn't.

2. The short version

Lowtide has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no trackers and no servers of its own that receive your information. We don't collect, store, share or sell personal data from the app. Everything Lowtide does happens on your iPhone. On the App Store, Lowtide's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

3. What Lowtide is

Lowtide opens the public websites of Instagram (instagram.com) and YouTube (m.youtube.com) inside the app, in Apple's standard web view, and hides the parts built to keep you scrolling, such as Instagram Reels and Explore and YouTube Shorts. It does this on your iPhone, by changing how the page looks once it has loaded. It uses no private interface of these services and doesn't copy, save or re-host their content.

4. What we don't collect

We never receive or have access to:

  • your username or password for Instagram, YouTube, Google or any other site you open in Lowtide
  • your messages, posts, stories, comments, photos or videos
  • who you follow, who follows you, or your contacts
  • what you watch, search for or tap on
  • your location
  • your name, email address, phone number or any account identifier
  • advertising identifiers or device identifiers
  • usage statistics or crash reports

5. Instagram and YouTube

When you log in, you log in on the site's own page (for example Instagram's or Google's). Your login details and everything you do on those sites go directly between your iPhone and that service. They never pass through Lowtide.

Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google) are separate companies. They are responsible for the data you share with them, and their own privacy policies apply:

  • Instagram: privacycenter.instagram.com/policy
  • YouTube and Google: policies.google.com/privacy

They can see that you visit their sites, just as they would in Safari. Lowtide doesn't change their tracking.

6. Other sites

Lowtide can also open LinkedIn, X, Snapchat and a general web browser. These work the same way as Instagram and YouTube: you log in on the site's own page, login cookies stay on your iPhone, and Lowtide receives nothing. Lowtide may also load these sites in the background so they open quickly. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • LinkedIn: linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • X: x.com/en/privacy
  • Snapchat: values.snap.com/privacy/privacy-policy

The browser opens on Google search, so Google's privacy policy applies to your searches: policies.google.com/privacy. It blocks trackers and cookie banners on the websites you visit, on your iPhone, using filter lists that are built into the app. Lowtide doesn't download these lists while you browse, and it doesn't record which sites you visit. The lists are EasyList, EasyPrivacy, EasyList Dutch and the EasyList Cookie List, made by the EasyList authors (easylist.to) and used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence (creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

TestFlight is run by Apple. If you install a test version, Apple shares some details with us, such as the email address you were invited with (if you joined by email), crash reports and feedback you choose to send. Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy apply to this. We use it only to fix problems in the test version and delete it when testing ends.

7. Data stored only on your iPhone

Some data stays on your iPhone so Lowtide works. We can't read it.

  • Login cookies and website data for the sites you open in Lowtide, so you stay logged in. These are set by those sites.
  • Your settings, such as which filters are on.

Deleting Lowtide from your iPhone removes all of it.

8. Loading ahead

To open quickly, Lowtide starts loading the home pages of the apps on its home screen in the background when you open the app, before you tap them. Your iPhone then connects to those sites as a browser would, so they receive the usual technical details of a web visit, such as your IP address.

9. Version check

When Lowtide opens, and when you come back to it, it downloads a small public file (status.json) from the Lowtide website. This tells the app whether your version still works or needs an update. It's the same file for everyone, and Lowtide adds nothing about you or your iPhone to the request.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may briefly log technical details of the request, such as your IP address, to run and protect its service. We don't receive these logs and don't use them to identify anyone. GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The same applies when you visit the Lowtide website in a browser. The website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

10. Links that open outside the feed

Some links, for example to other websites, open in an Apple Safari view inside Lowtide. Safari's own privacy settings apply there, and Lowtide can't see what you do in it.

11. Camera, microphone and photos

Lowtide only asks for camera, microphone or photo access when you use an Instagram feature that needs it, for example to post a photo, send a voice message or join a call. iOS asks you first, and you can change your answer at any time in the iPhone Settings app. The access is used by Instagram's own page to do what you asked. Lowtide doesn't record, read or upload anything itself.

12. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you (legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep the conversation for up to 12 months after it ends, unless the law requires longer, and then delete it. We don't add you to any mailing list. Our email provider stores the messages for us.

13. Purchases

Lowtide is currently free and has no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated before anything is sold.

14. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because the app collects nothing, we normally hold nothing about you except emails you sent us and, for test versions, what Apple shares through TestFlight. To use any of these rights, email himbelton@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You can also go to the authority in the EU country where you live.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law: we don't sell or share personal information and don't use it for targeted advertising.

15. Children

Lowtide is rated 16+ on the App Store and is not meant for children under 16. The sites have their own minimum ages. The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

16. Security

Lowtide's connections to Instagram, YouTube and the Lowtide website use encrypted HTTPS. Data saved on your iPhone is protected by iOS. No method is perfectly secure, but collecting nothing is the best protection we can offer.

17. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If Lowtide ever starts collecting personal data, we'll say so in the app before it happens and update the App Store privacy label first.
