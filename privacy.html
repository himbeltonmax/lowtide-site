Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

Lowtide is a phone app by Max Zijlstra, trading as Lowtide, in the Netherlands ("we"). Contact: himbelton@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Lowtide has no account and collects no personal data. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Instagram

You log in on Instagram's own page. Your login and everything you do on Instagram go between your phone and Instagram, and Instagram's privacy policy applies: privacycenter.instagram.com/policy.

On your phone

Your Instagram login and your Lowtide settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting Lowtide removes them.

Updates

Lowtide checks this website for update notices. The request contains no personal data. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no cookies.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold, which is normally none. Email himbelton@gmail.com. You can also contact the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Lowtide is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if this policy changes.

Lowtide is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
