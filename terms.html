2. What Lowtide does

Lowtide is a focus tool. It opens Instagram's public website inside the app and hides parts of it that are built to keep you scrolling. In version 1.0 these are the Reels tab, the Explore feed (search still works) and suggested posts; your feed shows the accounts you follow.

Lowtide also shows a short breathing moment when you open it, and a swipe from the bottom of the screen brings you back to Lowtide's home screen.

Lowtide is not the official Instagram app. It doesn't change Instagram's servers, app or content. It only changes how its web pages look on your iPhone. Not every Instagram feature may work inside Lowtide.
